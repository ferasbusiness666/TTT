TERMS OF USE

The rules for using Tadele Teen Talks, written the way we would say them.

These are the rules for using Tadele Teen Talks. We have tried to write them the way we would explain them out loud, rather than the way a contract usually reads.

Who we are

Tadele Teen Talks is an independent publication run by teenagers, sharing stories, opinions and creative work from young voices. It is not a company, a school publication, or a news agency.

The writing and artwork belong to the people who made it

Every story, photograph, video and illustration on this site belongs to the contributor who created it. They have given us permission to publish it here. That permission does not extend to you.

You are very welcome to read, link to, and share our work. Quoting a short passage with credit and a link is fine and encouraged. Republishing a whole piece, using our photographs or artwork elsewhere, or using anything here to train a commercial model, is not — ask us first at feroomon10400@gmail.com.

Using the site

Please do not attack the site, try to break into it, scrape it in bulk, or use it to send spam. Please do not misuse the newsletter form by entering addresses that are not yours. These are the only rules, and they exist because we are a small team and cleaning up after this comes out of the time we would rather spend writing.

There are no reader accounts

You do not need an account to read anything here, and you cannot create one. The sign-in screen is for our own editorial team only.

What we publish is opinion

Our writers are teenagers writing about their own lives and views. Their pieces are their own opinions, not statements of fact and not professional advice — not medical, legal, financial, or otherwise. If something here matters to a real decision you are making, please talk to someone qualified.

We try hard to be accurate. If we get something wrong, tell us and we will correct it.

Links to other places

We sometimes link to other websites and embed videos. We do not control those, and linking to something is not an endorsement of everything on it.

We publish this as best we can

We run this site carefully but we are a small volunteer team. We cannot promise the site will always be available or completely free of mistakes, and we are not able to take responsibility for losses arising from using it.

Changes

If these terms change we will update this page and the date at the bottom.

Contact

Anything at all — feroomon10400@gmail.com.
